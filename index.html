Understanding Stream & Reservoir Intel

Wading safety, water clarity, and temperature guidelines

Stoplight Volatility System

Green (< 5% Daily Change) — Prime & Stable

Flow and gauge depth have remained steady over 24 hours. Clarity is typically optimal and wading conditions are consistent.

Yellow (5.1% – 15% Change) — Shifting Water

Moderate flow adjustment or reservoir release. Watch for stained water, drifting debris, and slightly increased wading resistance.

Red (> 15% Change) — Volatile / Caution

High surge, blowout, or rapid drawdown. Wading can quickly become dangerous; stream bottom visibility drops significantly.

Rapid Rise Threshold (≥ 10%)

If flow rises by 10% or more between consecutive telemetry updates, an amber/red flash banner activates. This catches sudden dam gate releases (like Beaver or Platoro) or heavy flash rainstorms in the mountains.

48°F – 64°F Prime Feeding
