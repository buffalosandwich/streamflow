Understanding River & Pool Conditions

Stable (< 5% shift) Steady river flow or pool level. Predictable wading, clear visibility, and minimal floating debris.
Shifting (5% - 15% shift) Moderate rise or drop. Fish move toward banks; monitor footing while wading.
Volatile (> 15% shift) Surging flows or rapid drawdowns. Murky water, hazardous wading, and heavy drift.
