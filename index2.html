Understanding River & Pool Conditions

Green (< 5% change) Stable streamflow or pool. Predictable wading conditions, clear water, and safe fishing.
Yellow (5% - 15% change) Shifting conditions. Flow or storage is transitioning; monitor footing and bank water.
Red (> 15% change) Volatile water. Rapid rise or drawdown; expect murkiness, debris drift, and hazardous wading.
