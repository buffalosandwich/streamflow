How to read this page

24-hour change, hourly rise, temperature, reservoirs

24-hour stoplight

Color is the percent change versus the reading about 24 hours earlier. It is not a wading permit. On a small creek, a few cfs can be a big percent. Read the cfs (or acre-feet) next to the percent.

Green · under 5%. Volume is steady.
Yellow · 5% through 15%. Shifting. Exactly 15% stays yellow.
Red · over 15%. Volatile. Up is not treated as “good.”

The top counts split those three groups. A station only counts if a real reading exists within 6 hours of the 24-hour mark. A short record is not called “last week.”

Rapid rise

Separate from the 24-hour color. It fires only when the latest value is at least 10% higher than the reading about one hour earlier, and that earlier point falls between 35 and 85 minutes back. A multi-hour gap is not called an hourly jump. The same rule is used for river flow and reservoir storage. A drop never counts as a rise.

Water temperature

Live temperature uses DWR parameter WATTEMP, already in °F. There is no stand-in number. Only Beaver Creek below the reservoir, Conejos below Platoro, Rio Grande at Thirty Mile, and Rio Grande at Wagon Wheel Gap have a probe in this list. Everyone else says “No probe.”

  • Under 45°F · cold / slow
  • 45–48°F · cool
  • 48–64°F · prime feeding
  • 65–67°F · warm caution
  • Over 67°F · thermal stress. Consider not fishing.

Reservoirs

Percent full uses the capacity printed on the card. That capacity is a reference number stored in this page, not a live DWR field. Staff gage is a local pool stage. It is not feet above sea level, and it is not the dam crest. Platoro is the exception in this list: its elevation reading is actual pool elevation (about 10,000 ft). The approximate site elevation under a name is only a location hint.

Chart and missing data

The line is hourly for the last 72 hours. It is not one dot per day, so a release that lasts a morning still shows up. The 24-hour and 7-day comparisons stay in the sentence. If DWR has no reading, the card says so. Sample data is off unless you turn it on, and it is labeled on every card.
